The same habits at every depth.

  1. 0 m

    On the boat

    Reef-safe sunscreen only, and sulfate-free soap for your mask. The briefing covers every hand signal and every rule below.

  2. 3 m

    On the way down

    We check your buoyancy over sand, well before the reef comes into view.

  3. 12 m

    On the reef

    Look, don't touch. No chasing, no feeding, fins clear of the coral.

  4. 5 m

    Safety stop

    Three minutes, hovering, nothing to hold on to. Nothing leaves the water.

The short answers.

  • Do I need to be certified?

    Not always

    Not for Discover Scuba. Reef dives need a card, and the Blue Hole needs Advanced.

  • How old do I need to be?

    10 and up

    Younger children can snorkel with us.

  • How well do I need to swim?

    200 m

    For courses: 200 m, plus a 10-minute float.

  • What if the weather is bad?

    We reschedule

    We move your dive to another day, or refund it.

  • When can I fly?

    18 hours

    At least 18 hours after your last dive.

  • Is gear included?

    Mostly

    Tanks, weights and guide, yes. Mask, fins and wetsuit are rentals.
